Drinkroll is for adults of legal drinking age. It's a tool for keeping a group's tab and doesn't encourage drinking. Know your limits, drink water and never drink and drive.

Alcohol units are rough estimates based on typical drinks. They aren't a measure of blood alcohol, impairment or fitness to drive, and they aren't medical advice.
